Rules

  • Navy and white carry the page. Cyan is an accent, mostly on navy.
  • Never cyan text on white: it is too pale to read.
  • The logo is one word: WillisTemby, no space [G 29/10].
  • Isabella also liked the colour tones of atmosgroup.com.au [Q8].
Why these colours · PWD reasoning

Navy leads because it reads as established to CFOs and risk managers, and it moves the brand away from Miramar, which fills its homepage with large cyan shapes on white, and from Centrewest's navy with orange buttons. Keeping cyan small protects the "trusted Australian organisation" goal while still using the new logo. [P] [R] [G 29/10] [Q6]
